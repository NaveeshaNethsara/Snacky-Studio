How to download

  1. Paste a public video or photo-post link and select Continue or Get photo.
  2. A single photo offers Save photo and Save PNG. Photo collections offer Photos (ZIP). When TikTok's original music is available, choose Slideshow (MP4). Videos offer MP4 and MP3.
  3. Choose your save option. The input clears for your next link while Snacky Studio prepares the media locally. Follow the progress below; the finished file then saves through your browser.

In Chrome, open Downloads with Ctrl+J. If Chrome asks to allow multiple downloads, allow them for Snacky Studio. Keep the local server running until preparation finishes. Slideshow photos are evenly timed across the available music clip.
